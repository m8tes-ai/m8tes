- `client.model_connections.list()` returns each connected account's `state` (`ModelConnectionPlanState`): when the plan reopens (`reopens_at`, `reopens_source`), what it last refused (`last_refused_at`, `last_refused_reason`, `last_refused_model`), `refusals_14d` and `last_served_run_at`. `None`, or a zero count, means not known. The responses to connecting or disconnecting carry `state=None`.
