- `client.runs.wake(run_id)` starts a finished run's sandbox ahead of a reply, so the reply does not wait for the restore (36 to 61 seconds at the median). Call it when your user starts typing, and at most every 30 seconds while they go on. It is a hint: it returns `None` for any run you can read and starts nothing unless the run is finished, a person started it, its sandbox is resting, and the account can pay for the reply and has room. Pass `user_id` when the account has strict multi-tenant mode on.
