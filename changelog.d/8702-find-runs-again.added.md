- `client.runs.list(q=..., team_id=...)`: `q` finds a run again by mate name, task name, first request or conversation text (case-insensitive; mate names, task names and the first 200 characters of the first request are searched across the whole history, the full conversation text in the newest 1,000 runs of the rest of the filter, so pair it with `teammate_id` or `team_id` to reach further back). `team_id` keeps the runs of mates in a team or any team under it. Both survive `auto_paging_iter()`.
