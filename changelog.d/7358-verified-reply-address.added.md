- `client.account.add_reply_address(email)`, `list_reply_addresses()` and `remove_reply_address(id)` manage the addresses you write from besides the one on your account. Adding mails a confirmation link to the new address and stores nothing until its owner confirms; a confirmed address counts as you on a mate's own reply address, for a reply that names one of that mate's runs. Session-authenticated only: an `m8_` key gets a 403.
