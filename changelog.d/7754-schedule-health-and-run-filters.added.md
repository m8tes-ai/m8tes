- `client.runs.list(...)` takes `trigger_source` (`"schedule"`, `"manual"` or `"trigger"`), `created_after` (a `datetime` or an ISO 8601 string) and `started_by_teammate_id`. With `trigger_source="schedule"` and `created_after` it lists what a schedule ran today. `client.triggers.list(include_last_run=True)` fills `last_run` (a `TriggerLastRun`) and `failure_streak` on each schedule, and `Trigger.pause_reason` says why the system paused a schedule: `funding_required`, `model_connection_required` or `inactive_account`.
