- `client.billing.usage_breakdown(group_by="teammate" | "task")` says which mate or task uses your plan. Each group in `.groups` has `runs`, `delivered_runs`, `share_of_use` (a fraction of the window's plan use) and `median_run_tokens`; `.totals` covers the whole window. The window defaults to the last 7 UTC days and may be at most 93. A run counts once however many times it finished, and no dollar figure is returned.
